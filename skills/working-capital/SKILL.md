# Working Capital

Improve how cash moves through the ecommerce business.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather inventory balances, sales, COGS, AP, AR, supplier terms, payment processor lags, debt schedules, and cash forecast.

## Analyze

- Days inventory on hand.
- Inventory turns.
- Days payable outstanding.
- Days sales outstanding.
- Marketplace/payment processor settlement lag.
- Cash conversion cycle.
- Debt service and borrowing availability.

## Improvement Levers

- Supplier deposits and payment terms.
- PO timing and MOQ negotiation.
- Inventory liquidation or bundle strategy.
- Faster settlement or payment processor changes.
- AP scheduling.
- Debt refinancing or line utilization rules.

## Output

Return current working capital picture, biggest cash traps, recommended levers, and risks.
