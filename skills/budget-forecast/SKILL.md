# Budget and Forecast

Turn ecommerce goals into an operating plan that can be tracked.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Clarify the planning period, channels, product launches, inventory plans, hiring, debt, large payments, and owner goals.

## Build The Model

- Revenue by channel, product line, and month.
- Gross margin assumptions: product cost, freight, duties, merchant fees, fulfillment, returns, discounts.
- Marketing spend and expected contribution.
- Payroll and staffing plan.
- Fixed operating expenses and software.
- Inventory purchasing and working capital needs.
- Taxes, debt service, distributions, and capex.

## Budget Review

Compare actuals to budget using variance categories:

- Timing issue.
- Volume issue.
- Rate/cost issue.
- Mix issue.
- One-time issue.
- Accounting issue.

## Output

Return the budget structure, assumptions, variance commentary, and next management decisions.
