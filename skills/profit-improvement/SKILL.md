# Profit Improvement

Find practical profit levers and rank them by impact and effort.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather P&L, channel/product margins, ad spend, inventory reports, fulfillment costs, payroll, software, major obligations, and cash forecast.

## Profit Leak Scan

- Pricing and discounting.
- Product cost, freight, duties, tariffs, and packaging.
- Returns, refunds, chargebacks, and warranty costs.
- Fulfillment, shipping, and 3PL fees.
- Marketplace and merchant fees.
- Ad spend efficiency.
- Software and agency spend.
- Payroll and staffing.
- Inventory overstock and stockouts.
- Working capital and payment timing.

## Output

Return an impact/effort matrix, top five actions, estimated financial impact, data confidence, and owner-facing explanation.
