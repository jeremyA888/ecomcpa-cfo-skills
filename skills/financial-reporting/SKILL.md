# Financial Reporting

Create reporting that helps ecommerce leaders make decisions.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, sales/channel reports, inventory reports, forecast, and prior-month commentary.

## Report Pack

Include:

- Executive summary.
- Revenue by channel and product line.
- Gross margin and contribution margin.
- Operating expense trends.
- Cash, runway, working capital, debt, and major obligations.
- Inventory health: turns, stockouts, overstock, purchasing needs.
- KPI table with current period, prior period, target, and variance.
- Decisions needed from owner/leadership.

## Commentary Style

Explain what happened, why it happened, why it matters, and what to do next. Avoid accounting jargon unless the user asks for it.

## Output

Return a concise report outline or complete commentary with `Wins`, `Risks`, `Decisions`, and `Next Actions`.
