# Staffing Plan

Connect people decisions to capacity, profitability, and cash.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather current headcount, contractors, payroll costs, benefits, owner compensation, department owners, growth targets, fulfillment model, customer support volume, and cash forecast.

## Plan Structure

- Current payroll by department and role.
- Contractor and agency spend.
- Owner compensation and distributions.
- Required hires by trigger, not just date.
- Fully loaded cost: wages, employment burden, benefits, tools, recruiting, training.
- Capacity metrics: orders per ops employee, tickets per support rep, revenue per employee, finance close workload.
- Cash impact by month.

## Scenario Questions

- What breaks if the hire is delayed 90 days?
- What revenue or margin level justifies the hire?
- Is this a permanent role, fractional support, agency, or automation problem?
- Does the hire improve growth, reduce risk, or clean up operations?

## Output

Return a headcount plan, monthly payroll forecast, hiring triggers, affordability check, and owner-facing recommendation.
