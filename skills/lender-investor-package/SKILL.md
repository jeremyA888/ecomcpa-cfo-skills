# Lender and Investor Package

Prepare the financial evidence needed to fund ecommerce growth.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Clarify audience, purpose, amount requested, deadline, and decision criteria.

## Package Contents

- Executive summary.
- Historical P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow.
- Trailing twelve month view.
- Revenue, gross margin, contribution margin, and EBITDA trends.
- Cash flow forecast and use of funds.
- Inventory plan and purchase order schedule.
- Debt schedule and covenant metrics.
- Tax filing status and compliance notes.
- Risks and mitigants.

## Output

Return a document outline, required data list, KPI table, narrative summary, and likely questions from the lender/investor.
