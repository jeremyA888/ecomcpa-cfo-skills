# Product Margin

Find which products actually make money after ecommerce costs.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather SKU sales, units, discounts, refunds, product cost, freight/duties/tariffs, packaging, fulfillment, shipping, payment fees, returns, and ad allocation if available.

## Margin Ladder

Calculate:

- Selling price.
- Net revenue after discounts/refunds.
- Product cost.
- Landed cost.
- Fulfillment and shipping.
- Merchant/marketplace fees.
- Return cost.
- Contribution margin per unit and percent.

## Recommendations

Group products into:

- Scale.
- Fix price or discounting.
- Fix supplier/freight cost.
- Bundle differently.
- Liquidate or discontinue.

## Output

Return a ranked SKU table, margin drivers, data quality concerns, and decision recommendations.
