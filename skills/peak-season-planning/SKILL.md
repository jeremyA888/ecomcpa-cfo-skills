# Peak Season Planning

Prepare cash, inventory, staffing, margin, and reporting for high-volume periods.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather historical peak results, promo calendar, inventory on hand, open POs, supplier lead times, ad budget, staffing plan, cash forecast, and fulfillment capacity.

## Plan Areas

- Sales and margin forecast by channel.
- Inventory availability and reorder timing.
- Cash needed for deposits, freight, payroll, ads, and other large payments.
- Discount and promo contribution margin.
- Fulfillment capacity and surcharge risk.
- Daily/weekly KPI reporting cadence.

## Output

Return a peak-season finance plan, risk register, cash needs, daily metric list, and post-season close checklist.
