# Cash Flow Forecast

Forecast the cash needed to operate and grow an ecommerce brand.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Gather current cash, near-term AR/AP, inventory purchase plan, payroll, debt schedule, ad spend, sales forecast, gross margin, and owner distributions.

## Model Structure

- Beginning cash.
- Cash receipts by channel and settlement lag.
- Inventory purchases and freight.
- Payroll and contractor spend.
- Ads, fulfillment, software, rent, professional fees, and other operating expenses.
- Debt payments, interest, owner distributions, and capex.
- Ending cash and minimum cash threshold.

## Scenarios

Build at least base, downside, and growth cases. Stress test:

- Slower marketplace payouts.
- Lower conversion or higher ad costs.
- Larger inventory deposit.
- Large payment timing.
- Delayed supplier shipment.

## Output

Return the forecast assumptions, weekly or monthly cash table, lowest cash point, decision risks, and actions to improve cash.
