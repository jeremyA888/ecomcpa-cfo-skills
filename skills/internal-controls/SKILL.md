# Internal Controls

Reduce errors, fraud, and reporting risk without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Start Here

Read `.agents/ecom-finance.md` if it exists. Identify team roles, systems, payment methods, approval paths, inventory custody, and close process.

## Control Areas

- Bank, credit card, and payment processor access.
- Vendor setup and bill approval.
- Purchase orders and inventory receipts.
- Payroll changes and owner pay.
- Refunds, discounts, gift cards, and chargebacks.
- Journal entries and balance sheet reconciliations.
- System admin permissions and integrations.

## Output

Return a control matrix with risk, control, owner, cadence, evidence, and implementation priority.
